👨‍🏫 Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin Lamusse
1. Reverse Percentage (Q9)

Never take 20% off R240! That's a common trap. When an amount increases by 20%, R240 represents 120%. Divide by 120 and multiply by 100 to get back to 100%.

2. Midpoint Coordinates (Q10)

Mnemonic: "Add and halve!" Add the two x-numbers and divide by 2; add the two y-numbers and divide by 2.
