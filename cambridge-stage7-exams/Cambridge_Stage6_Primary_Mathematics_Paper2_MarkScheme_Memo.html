🎯 4 Tutor Coaching Tips for Justin (ADHD & Dyscalculia Friendly)
  1. Decimal Padding (Q1 & Q2): If you see \(15.3 - 6.84\), ALWAYS write a zero to make it \(15.30\). That way you can clearly borrow from 30 to make 10.
  2. Compound L-Shape Area (Q9): Slice the shape into two simple rectangles! Never try to do it in your head. Color or shade Rectangle 1 and Rectangle 2, find their areas, then add them.
  3. Scale Factors (Q14): Going from 4 people to 6 people is just adding half as much again! Flour: 200 + half (100) = 300g. Eggs: 2 + half (1) = 3 eggs.
  4. Litres to Millilitres (Q18): Never subtract ml from litres directly! Convert first: \(1.5\text{ L} = 1500\text{ ml}\). Then do simple column subtraction.
