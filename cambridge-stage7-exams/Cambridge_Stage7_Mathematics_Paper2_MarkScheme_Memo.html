👨‍🏫 Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin Lamusse
1. Calculator Syntax vs Order of Operations (Q1b, Q9b)

When computing with fractions or multiple terms on a calculator, remind Justin to use parentheses for numerators: (2 × 8 + 5) ÷ 3, rather than typing in sequence which might divide only the last term.

2. Unit Conversion Chains in Scale Drawings (Q5)

Coach Justin on the 2-step metric ladder: cm → m (÷ 100) → km (÷ 1000). Total division is by 100 000. Skipping steps leads to decimal place errors.

3. Area of Compound L-Shapes (Q4b)

Encourage Justin to physically draw a dotted line on the paper splitting the L into two distinct rectangles (Rectangle A and Rectangle B). Label the dimensions of each rectangle clearly before calculating areas to avoid using overlapping outer lengths.

4. Average Speed with Breaks (Q11b)

Ensure Justin calculates total distance divided by total elapsed time (including stationary delivery time), never averaging the separate speeds!
