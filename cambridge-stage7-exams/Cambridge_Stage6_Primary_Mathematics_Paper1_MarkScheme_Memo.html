🌟 Key to Cambridge Mark Types
M Marks: Method marks for showing correct mathematical working, even if arithmetic error occurs.
A Marks: Accuracy marks awarded for correct final answers following correct working.
B Marks: Independent marks awarded unconditionally for a correct fact or value.

1 (a) 634 B1 Column addition: \(358 + 276 = 634\). Watch carry over of 1 ten and 1 hundred.
1 (b) 315 B1 Borrowing across zero: \(502 - 187\). Turn 500 into 490 + 12. \(12 - 7 = 5\), \(9 - 8 = 1\), \(4 - 1 = 3\).

🎯 4 Tutor Coaching Tips for Justin (ADHD & Dyscalculia Friendly)
  1. Borrowing from Zero (Q1b): If you have 502, cross out 50 to make 49, and give the 1 to make 12. Don't do multiple chaotic steps.
  2. Thermometer Number Line (Q3): Always draw a vertical line with 0 in the middle. Going up is positive; going down is negative. -8 is lower than -5.
  3. Time Intervals (Q17): Bridge through the nearest hour! 14:35 → (25 mins) → 15:00 → (1 hr 10 mins) → 16:10. Total = 1 hr 35 min.
  4. Ratio Bar Box (Q12): Ratio 1:2 means 3 equal boxes. Total R60 ÷ 3 = R20 per box. Share 1 has 1 box (R20), Share 2 has 2 boxes (R40).
