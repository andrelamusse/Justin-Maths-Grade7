👨‍🏫 Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin Lamusse
1. Diameter vs Radius Trap in Circle Calculations (Q8)

The #1 Cambridge exam error is using diameter instead of radius in the area formula! Emphasize: Always write down r = d ÷ 2 = 7 cm immediately upon reading the word diameter.

2. Back-Bearings Calculation (Q10)

If forward bearing is < 180°, add 180°: 65° + 180° = 245°. If forward bearing is > 180°, subtract 180°. Always draw parallel North arrows at both points.

3. Identifying Prime Numbers (Q11b)

Remind Justin: 1 is NOT a prime number! The primes on a standard 6-sided die are 2, 3, and 5 (3 primes out of 6). 2 is the only even prime.
