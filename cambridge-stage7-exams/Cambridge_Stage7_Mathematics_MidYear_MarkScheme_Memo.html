👨‍🏫 Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin Lamusse
1. Double Negatives in Algebraic Substitution (Q6)

Coach Justin to evaluate exponents first: (−3)2 = +9 because a negative times a negative is positive! Common trap is calculating −32 = −9.

2. Negative Sign Expansion Trap (Q7a)

Watch out for −2(x − 5). Expanding gives −2x + 10 (not −10). Remind Justin that the minus sign outside the bracket flips all signs inside!

3. Area of a Trapezium (Q12)

Use the verbal mantra: "Add the parallel tracks, halve the answer, and multiply by the height." Drawing the two parallel lines in green helps visual learners avoid using slant side lengths.
