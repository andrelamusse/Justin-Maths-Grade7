Cambridge Assessment Marking Symbols Guide:
M Marks: Method marks for showing correct mathematical working steps (independent of arithmetic slips).
A Marks: Accuracy marks awarded only if the corresponding M mark has been earned.
B Marks: Unconditional independent marks awarded for correct answers, values, or statements.
ft: Follow-through marks awarded when subsequent working is correct following an earlier arithmetic slip.

💡 Teacher & Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin

Justin learns best with structured visual models, verbal walkthroughs, and zero cognitive clutter. When tutoring him through Paper 1:

1. Fractions (Q4) — Use the "Pizza Slices" Visual Bar Model

For \(\frac{3}{5} + \frac{1}{4}\), do not just say "cross multiply". Draw a bar divided into 20 equal boxes. Show that \(\frac{3}{5}\) is 12 boxes, and \(\frac{1}{4}\) is 5 boxes. Justin will immediately see \(12 + 5 = 17\) out of 20 boxes.

2. Ratios & Sharing (Q13) — The 3-Step "Boxes" Trick

Have Justin physically draw: 2 boxes for Brother A, 3 boxes for Brother B, 4 boxes for Brother C. Total = 9 boxes.
Ask: "If 9 boxes equal R180, what is inside 1 single box?" (Justin computes \(180 \div 9 = R20\)).
Then ask: "Youngest brother has 2 boxes. What is inside his 2 boxes?" (\(2 \times R20 = R40\)).

3. BIDMAS Order of Operations (Q2) — The Underline Habit

For \(18 - 3 \times (4 + 2)\), have Justin underline the brackets first: \(18 - 3 \times \underline{(4 + 2)}\) \(\to 18 - \underline{3 \times 6}\) \(\to 18 - 18 = 0\). This prevents the common ADHD impulse to subtract \(18 - 3 = 15\) first.

4. Sequences (Q8) — Finding the "+3" Zeroth Term

When Justin sees \(7, 11, 15, 19\), tell him: "It goes up by 4, so it belongs to the 4 times table (\(4n\)). Now step BACK one step before the start: \(7 - 4 = 3\). Put them together: \(4n + 3\)." He will never forget this formula trick.
