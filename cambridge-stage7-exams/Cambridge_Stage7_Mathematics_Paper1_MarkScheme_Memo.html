Cambridge Assessment Marking Symbols Guide:
M Marks: Method marks for showing correct mathematical working steps (independent of arithmetic slips).
A Marks: Accuracy marks awarded only if the corresponding M mark has been earned.
B Marks: Unconditional independent marks awarded for correct answers, values, or statements.
ft: Follow-through marks awarded when subsequent working is correct following an earlier arithmetic slip.

👨‍🏫 Tutor Pedagogical Coaching Guide for Justin Lamusse
1. Sign Errors with Negative Integers (Q1)

Remind Justin to use the Integer Thermometer mental model. For addition −7 + 12, visualize starting in freezing territory (−7) and rising 12 degrees up past zero to reach +5. For multiplication and division, enforce the rhythmic mantra: "Same signs make positive, different signs make negative."

2. BIDMAS Precedence Traps (Q2)

Dyscalculic students frequently read left-to-right and compute 18 − 3 = 15 before multiplying. Coach Justin to draw physical brackets around multiplication blocks: 18 − [3 × 6] before performing subtraction.

3. Keep-Change-Flip Fraction Division (Q4c)

Use the mnemonic KFC (Keep, Flip, Change). Keep the first fraction (4/9), flip the second to its reciprocal (3/2), and change ÷ to ×. Emphasize cross-cancelling before multiplying numerators and denominators to prevent large numbers.

4. Metric Unit Ratio Conversion (Q13a)

Justin must always check units before writing a ratio! 40 cm to 1.2 m cannot be written as 40 : 1.2. Always convert the larger unit into the smaller unit: 1.2 m × 100 = 120 cm, giving 40 : 120 = 1 : 3.
